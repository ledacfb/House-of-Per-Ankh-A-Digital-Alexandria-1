Ananda · Meditation & Self-Realization

Yogananda, contemplative practice, and the Ananda tradition

Before you begin

This collection focuses on Ananda Sangha, the modern spiritual movement founded by Swami Kriyananda and inspired by Paramhansa Yogananda. It includes freely accessible reading and practice resources. Spiritual experiences and metaphysical teachings are presented as tradition-specific perspectives.
