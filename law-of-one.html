Introduction

Understanding the Law of One

The Law of One is a spiritual philosophy centered on the idea that all existence originates from, and remains connected through, one infinite consciousness or Creator. Every being, experience, and form of life participates in a larger process of spiritual evolution.

Although individuals experience themselves as separate, the teachings propose that all creation is fundamentally interconnected. The Law of One invites us to reflect on compassion, personal responsibility, free will, and our relationship to the greater whole.

❀

A note for the seeker: The Ra Contact is modern channeled spiritual material. Its metaphysical claims are presented here for philosophical and spiritual exploration, not as independently verified science or history.

Origins & History

The Ra Material

Between 1981 and 1984, Don Elkins, Carla Rueckert, and Jim McCarty conducted 106 recorded channeling sessions with an intelligence identifying itself as Ra. These sessions became known as The Law of One or The Ra Material and were published and preserved by L/L Research.

In the transcripts, Ra describes itself as a sixth-density collective consciousness, or “social memory complex,” and refers to a Confederation of spiritual beings. These descriptions belong to the channeled narrative, rather than verified historical accounts.
