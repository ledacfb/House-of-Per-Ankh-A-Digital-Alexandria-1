Welcome to the Study

A Living Library

Amura is the creator and keeper of the House of Life Mystery Schools — a living digital sanctuary dedicated to the preservation, exploration, and contemplation of sacred, philosophical, and esoteric traditions.

The House of Life was created as a growing library rather than a completed archive. Its Chambers will continue to unfold over time through texts, teachings, films, reflection, and study.

This study will grow with the library. Future collections can include Amura's welcome message, original writings, films, reflections, and the story of the Order of the Golden Dragon.
