The Akashic Records

Akasha · Theosophy · Modern Esoteric Traditions

Before you begin

Akasha is a term with roots in South Asian philosophical traditions, while the idea of an accessible universal record was elaborated in modern Theosophical and esoteric writing. These sources document spiritual beliefs and literary traditions; the existence of a literal cosmic record has not been scientifically established. All four linked readings are free to access.
