The Library Covenant

Free to Enter. Careful With Sources. Always Growing.

Open Access

No account, subscription, or payment is required to explore House of Life.

Source Respect

Public-domain and authorized materials may be hosted or linked. Copyrighted works are linked to lawful sources rather than copied.

Clear Context

Historical traditions, spiritual interpretations, and channelled or claimed material are identified as such rather than presented as established scientific fact.

Living Archive

New books, documents, lectures, films, and connections between chambers can be added over time.
