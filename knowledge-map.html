Suggested Journeys

Don't know where to begin?

I want to understand consciousness.

Seth → Law of One → Ananda. Compare a channeled psychology of self, a channeled cosmology of consciousness, and a contemplative practice tradition.

I want to explore ancient and esoteric philosophy.

Thoth → Sacred Geometry → Ascended Masters. Start with Hermetic sources, separate mathematics from symbolism, then examine modern Theosophical developments.

I want to study modern channeled literature.

Seth → Law of One → Galactic Federation. Compare chronology, methods, vocabulary, authorship, and claims rather than assuming similarity proves a shared source.

I want a practice, not only ideas.

Ananda → Thoth → Law of One. Begin with meditation resources, then examine how other collections frame self-knowledge and transformation.
