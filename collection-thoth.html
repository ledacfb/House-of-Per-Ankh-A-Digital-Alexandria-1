Thoth & Hermetic Wisdom

Ancient Hermetica and modern Hermetic interpretations

Before you begin

Begin with the late-antique Hermetic dialogues, then compare them with the modern 1908 work The Kybalion. These writings come from different periods and should not be treated as a single historical text.

All reading links lead to freely accessible, source-hosted material. No account is needed to use House of Life.
