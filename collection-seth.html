Before you begin

Jane Roberts (1929–1984) presented the Seth Material through trance sessions beginning in the 1960s, recorded with Robert Butts. The works are modern channeled literature, not independently verified accounts of a nonphysical entity. This reading path links to free introductory material and bibliographic sources; full copyrighted books may require borrowing or purchase.
