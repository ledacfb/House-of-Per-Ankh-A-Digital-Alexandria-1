Ascended Masters · History & Traditions

Theosophy, Mahatmas, and later ascended-master movements

Before you begin

“Ascended Masters” is a term associated especially with modern esoteric movements. This path begins with historical Theosophical sources about Mahatmas and proceeds to later interpretations. Claims of supernatural teachers or communications are spiritual beliefs, not independently established facts.
